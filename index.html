FAQ

Before you book.

Are you mobile?

No. Services are currently performed at the detailing location by appointment. Exact drop-off information is provided after booking.

How do I get a price?

Send a DM with your vehicle's year, make, model, condition and the service you're interested in. Some services vary based on size and condition.

What is paint correction?

Paint correction uses machine polishing to improve clarity and reduce visible defects such as swirls, haze and oxidation. Results depend on paint condition and the service selected.

Do you work on trucks and SUVs?

Yes. Send the vehicle information in your quote request so pricing and timing can be confirmed.

How long does a detail take?

Timing depends on the service and vehicle condition. The expected turnaround is confirmed when the appointment is booked.
